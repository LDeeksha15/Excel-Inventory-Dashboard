## Overview

The Inventory Management Dashboard is an interactive Excel-based business intelligence solution designed to analyze inventory performance, sales trends, demand forecasting, pricing strategies, and discount effectiveness.

Built using Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers, the dashboard transforms raw operational data into actionable insights, enabling data-driven inventory and sales decisions.

## Key Highlights

- Analyzed 73K+ inventory and sales records
- Developed 6 interactive visualizations
- Implemented 5 dynamic slicers for real-time filtering
- Tracked inventory levels across stores and categories
- Compared demand forecasts with actual sales performance
- Analyzed pricing and discount impact on sales
- Identified sales trends across multiple years

## Dashboard Components

### Inventory Level by Store and Category
Visualizes inventory distribution across stores and product categories.

### Units Sold Trend Over Time
Tracks monthly and yearly sales performance.

### Demand Forecast vs Units Sold
Compares forecasted demand with actual product sales.

### Average Price by Product Category
Analyzes pricing trends across categories.

### Discount Impact on Sales
Evaluates the relationship between discounts and sales performance.

### Sales by Store and Category
Identifies top-performing stores and categories.
